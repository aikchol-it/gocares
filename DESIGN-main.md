# Design System: Aikchol Hospital (AHC)

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

UI/UX design for Aikchol Hospital centers around the core mission: **"Enhancing Family Happiness."** The design philosophy creates a warm, caring, approachable, and highly professional digital environment that instills medical trust and promotes family well-being.

### Core Brand Values

* **Happiness:** Conveyed through cheerful family imagery and deep, reassuring ocean blues.
* **Caring:** Expressed through thoughtful layout flows, attentive service highlights, and warm golden accents.
* **Warmth:** Embedded in approachable typography and vibrant accent elements.
* **Trustworthy:** Grounded in structured corporate navigation and clean clinical cyan-green indicators.

## 3. Typography Rules

### Primary Font Family

* **`Noto Sans Thai Looped`**: Standardized font family across all digital touchpoints to ensure legibility, clarity, and modern accessibility.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Weights

* **Main Headings (H1, H2):** Noto Sans Thai Looped Medium
  * Usage: Hero titles, primary page headers, major section titles.
* **Sub-headings (H3, H4):** Noto Sans Thai Looped Regular
  * Usage: Component headers, card titles, subsection headers.
* **Body Content:** Noto Sans Thai Looped Light
  * Usage: Paragraph text, article content, form labels, and standard UI instructions.

---

## 4. Component Stylings & Principles

* **Navigation Bar & Header:**
  * Grounded in **AHC-BLUE** (`#004680`) to reinforce brand consistency and user confidence upon entry.
* **Buttons & Call-to-Actions (CTAs):**
  * Primary buttons utilize **AHC-BLUE** (`#004680`) to signal key interactions and establish clear visual affordance.
* **Care & Personalized Service Badges:**
  * Highlighted using **AHC-YELLOW** (`#FFB81C`) to draw attention to personal care features and supportive healthcare services.
* **Medical Cards & Clinical Indicators:**
  * Accented with **AHC-GREEN** (`#2DCCD3`) to delineate medical categories, health metrics, and active clinical status updates.

---

## 5. Layout & Emotional Design Principles

* **Emotional Design:** Incorporate vibrant, high-quality imagery featuring happy families and healthy lifestyles to align visually with the brand's core mission.
* **Visual Consistency:** Maintain structured padding, clear typography hierarchy, and consistent color mapping across all patient and public-facing screens.
